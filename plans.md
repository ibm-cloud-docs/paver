# Paver plans
{: #paver-plans}

Compare Paver plans to choose the tier that fits your team's needs, from the free plan for individual exploration to the enterprise plan for large-scale operations.
{: shortdesc}

Paver is available in three plans: Free, Professional, and Enterprise. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation for running Paver operations. When credits are exhausted, the behavior depends on your plan. Storage, available features, and billing entity differ across plans.

Credits are consumed as you use Paver. The cost per credit decreases at higher tiers.

Future consideration: prepaid **credit packs** — buy credits ahead at a discount — are planned but not yet available.
{: note}

## Plan details
{: #paver-plan-details}

Free
:   The Free plan is designed for individual exploration and learning. You receive 1,000 credits per month at no cost. When credits are exhausted, Paver enters support mode, meaning it can continue to provide information and guidance but cannot take actions on your behalf until credits renew. Storage is limited to 10 MB, and you can add up to three user skills.

Professional
:   The Professional plan is designed for individual practitioners and small teams who run regular operations. For $250 per month, you receive 10,000 credits. When credits are exhausted, additional usage is billed pay-as-you-go at $0.015 per credit. Storage increases to 50 MB, and there is no limit on user skills.

Enterprise
:   The Enterprise plan is designed for teams and organizations running Paver at scale. For $2,500 per month, you receive 200,000 credits, with overage billed at $0.01 per credit. Billing is managed at the account hierarchy level, with downstream entitlement flowing from the top parent account. Storage is 1 GB. The Enterprise plan also unlocks enterprise skills, enterprise memory, and — when available at GA — headless operation and Slack and Microsoft Teams integration.
