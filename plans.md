# Paver plans
{: #paver-plans}

Paver has a Free and Professional plan. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation for running Paver operations. Credits are consumed as you use Paver, and when credits are exhausted, the behavior depends on your plan. Credits reset each month. For full details on pricing and included features per plan, go to [TBD](TBD). 

Free
:   The Free plan is designed for individual exploration and learning. You receive a set amount of credits per month at no cost. When credits are exhausted, Paver enters read-only mode, meaning it can continue to provide information and guidance but can't' take actions on your behalf until credits renew. Supported features may vary.

Professional
:   The Professional plan is designed for individual practitioners and teams who run regular operations. When credits are exhausted, additional usage is billed on a pay-as-you-go basis per credit.

You can upgrade from the Free plan to the Professional plan at any time. If you are reaching your limit on the Free plan, you are prompted in the console with alerts that you're approaching your limit, and you can choose to upgrade at that time.

1. Go to ![Help icon](../icons/help.svg "Help") **Help** menu > **Chat with Paver**.
1. Click ![Settings](../icons/settings.svg "Settings icon") **Settings**, then click **All settings**.
1. Go to **Billing and usage**.
1. Click **Upgrade** to review the plan options and upgrade.
