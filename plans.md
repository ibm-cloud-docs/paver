# Paver plans
{: #paver-plans}

Compare Paver plans to choose the tier that fits your team's needs, from the free plan for individual exploration to the professional plan for regular operations.
{: shortdesc}

Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation for running Paver operations. When credits are exhausted, the behavior depends on your plan. Storage and available features differ across plans. Credits are consumed as you use Paver.

## Plan details
{: #paver-plan-details}

Paver for use in {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} is available by using one of the following plans:

Free
:   The Free plan is designed for individual exploration and learning. You receive 1,000 credits per month at no cost. When credits are exhausted, Paver enters support mode, meaning it can continue to provide information and guidance but cannot take actions on your behalf until credits renew. Storage is limited to 10 MB, and you can add up to three user skills.

Professional
:   The Professional plan is designed for individual practitioners and teams who run regular operations. For $250 per month, you receive 10,000 credits. When credits are exhausted, additional usage is billed on a pay-as-you-go basis at $0.015 per credit. Storage increases to 50 MB, and there is no limit on user skills.
