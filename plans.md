# Paver plans in {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}}
{: #paver-plans}

{{site.data.keyword.paver_full}} has a Free and Professional plan. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation for running operations with {{site.data.keyword.paver}}. Credits are consumed as you use {{site.data.keyword.paver}}, and when credits are exhausted, the behavior depends on your plan. Credits reset each month.
{: shortdesc}

Free
:   The Free plan is designed for individual exploration and learning. You receive a set number of credits per month at no cost. When credits are exhausted, {{site.data.keyword.paver}} enters read-only mode, meaning it can continue to provide information and guidance but can't act on your behalf until credits renew. Supported features might vary.

Professional
:   The Professional plan is designed for individual practitioners and teams who run regular operations. When credits are exhausted, additional usage is billed on a pay-as-you-go basis per credit.

For full details on pricing and included features per plan, go to ![Help icon](../icons/help.svg "Help") **Help** menu > **Paver AI assistant**, and then click ![Settings](../icons/settings.svg "Settings icon") **Settings** > **All settings** > **Billing**.



## Switching plans for {{site.data.keyword.paver}}
{: #switching-plans}

You can switch from the Free plan to the Professional plan at any time. If you are reaching your limit while you are using the Free plan, you are alerted in the console that you're approaching your limit, and you can choose to upgrade at that time.

1. Go to ![Help icon](../icons/help.svg "Help") **Help** menu > **Ask Paver**.
1. Click ![Settings](../icons/settings.svg "Settings icon") **Settings**, then click **All settings**.
1. Go to **Billing**.
1. Review the available plans and select the option that works best for you.
1. Agree to the terms and conditions, and click **Confirm**.
