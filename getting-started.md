# Getting started with IBM Paver
{: #getting-started}
{: toc-content-type="tutorial"}
{: toc-completion-time="10m"}

Learn how to get started with Paver by using the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console.
{: shortdesc}

IBM Paver is available in the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console, where you can quickly start a chat with Paver to manage your account and resources and operate your infrastructure. To interact with Paver from your terminal instead, see [Installing the Paver CLI](/docs/paver?topic=paver-paver-cli-install).

## Access Paver in the console
{: #paver-console}
{: step}

You can chat with Paver directly in the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console.

1. Log in to your {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} account.
1. From the {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} console menu bar, click ![Help icon](../icons/help.svg "Help") > **Chat with Paver**. 

Note the mode switcher where you can switch between Safe and Edit mode. Since you're just getting started, stay in Safe mode for now, which will keep Paver from taking any actions on your behalf until you're ready.
{: tip}

## Take the getting started tour
{: #tour}
{: step}

1. From IBM Paver in the console, click ![Help icon](../icons/help.svg "Help") > **Getting started tour**.
1. Follow the prompts to to tell Paver a little bit about yourself and try out a prompt for the first time.


## Start a new conversation
{: #paver-interactive}
{: step}

Start a new conversation and explore how Paver can help.

1. Select **New task** to start a new conversation. After completing the getting started tour,
1. Choose a starter prompt from Getting started, Create, or Investigate, or type your own question. Use natural language to describe what you want to do or what you want to know.
1. Click **Send message** to start the conversation.

You can ask Paver to tell you about it's capabilities and features to learn about how Paver can assist. It has knowledge of your account and resources, so it can help you troubleshoot, gain insight, or accomplish a task.
{: tip}

## Tell Paver about yourself
{: #personalize-workflow}
{: step}

Paver remembers things about you across conversations such as your preferred regions, how you like to work, the projects you're running, and other context that makes future interactions faster and more relevant. This is called memory in Paver. You can explicitly teach Paver about your preferences by telling Paver directly or try out the `/personalize` slash command to have Paver prompt you for answers to questions about how you like to work:

1. In the chat, enter `/personalize`. This starts an interactive interview to help Paver learn more about your preferences.
1. Respond to Paver as it asks you questions to build new memories.


## Explore the settings
{: #paver-settings}
{: step}

Set your theme, language preference, and Paver Guard approval policies to put guardrails in place and control how often you're asked to approve specific actions.

1. Click ![Settings](../icons/settings.svg "Settings icon") **Settings**.
1. Set your theme to match how you like to work.
1. Set your preferred language.
1. Review the auto-approve and auto-deny options. For more information about setting guard policies, see [Managing safeguards and controls](https://paver.ibm.com/docs/core-concepts/managing-risk-and-control).
1. Select **All settings** explore options for managing memories and storing secrets in the keyring.

## Next steps
{: #next-steps}
{: step}

Now that you know how to find Paver and start a chat, you can explore all of the possibilities by chatting directly with Paver. Paver is your best guide to understand all of its capabilities and making a plan to streamline your infrastructure operations.

You can also leverage the following resources to learn more about Paver:
* For more information about Paver and its core concepts and tasks, see the [IBM Paver](https://paver.ibm.com/docs/welcome){: external} docs.
* Decide which [plan](/docs/paver?topic=paver-paver-plans) works best for you.
* [Install Paver CLI](/docs/paver?topic=paver-paver-cli-install) to work with Paver in your terminal.
